Research

All themes →

Citizen-centric multi-agent systems

AI that makes good collective decisions when people have competing interests, and that those people can understand and trust.

Incentives & mechanism design

Rules for allocation and pricing that hold up when people and software agents act strategically.

Learning preferences under uncertainty

Learning what people want and acting on it over time, from preference elicitation to reinforcement learning.

Trustworthy & accountable agents

Responsibility, trust and ethical alignment in multi-agent systems, including LLM-based agents.
